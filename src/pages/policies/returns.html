Last updated 10 October 2026

Your right to cancel

If you are a consumer you may cancel most orders within 14 days of receiving them, for any reason, and return them within a further 14 days. We refund the price of the goods and the standard outbound delivery cost within 14 days of receiving the goods back.

Sealed products

Sealed product returned under the right to cancel must still be sealed. If it has been opened we may reduce the refund to reflect the loss in value, because opened trading-card product cannot be resold as sealed.

Faulty, damaged, tampered or incorrect items

Tell us within a reasonable time and we will put it right by replacement or refund. This is separate from the right to cancel and is not affected by it.

Unboxing video and photographs

We strongly encourage you to record one continuous video as you open your parcel, showing the unopened package, the shipping label, the packaging and the contents. For claims involving damage, tampering, missing products, missing items, incorrect orders or other delivery-related disputes, we may ask to see that video and any photographs to help investigate.

Not having an unboxing video does not automatically remove your statutory consumer rights. We will still investigate any claim using the packing photograph we hold, courier records and any other evidence available.

Pre-orders

You may cancel a pre-order for a full refund at any time before it is dispatched. If a release date moves we will email you and you can cancel then.

Return postage

You pay return postage when you are exercising the right to cancel. We pay it when the item is faulty, damaged, incorrect or not as described.

How to start a return

Contact us with your order number. Do not send anything back before we reply — we will confirm the return address and how to package it.
