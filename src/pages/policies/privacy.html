Our approach to privacy

We aim to collect and use only the personal information reasonably necessary to operate our website, process orders, deliver products, respond to enquiries, prevent fraud and meet our legal obligations.

We do not intend to sell customers' personal information to advertisers.

The website is designed to operate without requiring customers to create an account. However, information may still be processed by our website host, payment providers, delivery partners and other services used to operate the website.

The exact information processed depends on the features and services used during your visit or purchase.

Information we may collect

Information you provide

When you place an order, contact us or submit a wholesale enquiry, we may collect:

  • Your name.
  • Email address.
  • Billing and delivery addresses.
  • Telephone number, where requested or needed for delivery.
  • Order details, products purchased and order reference.
  • Messages and information you choose to provide.

  • Photographs, videos and other evidence you voluntarily provide when reporting damage, delivery problems or another issue.

Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is genuinely necessary for your enquiry.

Payment information

Payments may be processed by Stripe or another payment provider made available at checkout.

The relevant payment provider may collect payment-card details, billing information, contact details, fraud-prevention information and other information required to authorise or process a transaction.

Where payment details are collected directly by the provider, we do not intentionally receive or store your full payment-card number or card security code on our own systems.

We may receive information such as payment status, transaction reference, the amount paid and information necessary to fulfil your order, issue a refund or handle a payment dispute.

Payment providers may process personal information under their own privacy notices and, depending on the activity, may act as independent data controllers.

Website and technical information

When you visit our website, our hosting provider and relevant technical services may automatically process information such as:

  • IP address.
  • Browser and device information.
  • Operating system.
  • Requested pages and resources.
  • Date and time of requests.
  • Security and error logs.
  • Information about website performance and attempted misuse.

This information may be used to operate the website, maintain security, diagnose technical issues and prevent abuse.

Our website may also store shopping-basket information and cookie or consent preferences in your browser. The specific storage technologies used will depend on the website implementation.

Contact and trade forms

If you contact us through a website form or by email, we process the information you submit so that we can respond to your request.

Where our forms are provided by Netlify or another third-party service, your submission may be processed or stored by that provider and transmitted to us.

We use this information to answer questions, handle orders, respond to trade enquiries and maintain relevant business records.

How and why we use personal information

We use personal information for the following purposes.

Order processing and delivery: to process payments, confirm orders, arrange dispatch, deliver goods, communicate delivery updates and manage returns or refunds.

Customer support: to respond to questions, investigate complaints, resolve delivery problems and provide after-sales support.

Wholesale enquiries: to assess and respond to trade enquiries, discuss product availability and pricing, and administer any business-to-business arrangements.

Payment security and fraud prevention: to protect our business and customers, investigate suspected fraudulent activity, prevent misuse and handle payment disputes or chargebacks.

Website operation and security: to maintain website availability, diagnose errors, protect against unauthorised access and improve reliability.

Legal and accounting obligations: to maintain appropriate business records, comply with tax and accounting requirements, respond to lawful requests and establish or defend legal claims.

We do not intend to use customers' personal information for behavioural advertising or sell it to third parties for their independent advertising purposes.

Our lawful bases for processing

Under applicable UK data-protection law, we must have a lawful basis for processing personal information.

Depending on the purpose, we rely on the following bases:

Contractual necessity: we process relevant information where it is necessary to enter into or perform a contract with you, such as processing an order, arranging delivery or administering a refund.

Legitimate interests: we may process information where necessary for our legitimate business interests, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights and interests. These interests may include website security, fraud prevention, handling enquiries, defending legal claims and managing business operations.

Legal obligation: we process and retain information where necessary to comply with legal obligations, including applicable tax, accounting and record-keeping requirements.

Consent: where consent is legally required, such as for certain non-essential cookies or electronic marketing activities, we will request consent before carrying out the relevant processing.

You may withdraw consent at any time where processing relies on consent. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

Where we rely on legitimate interests, you can ask us for further information about those interests.

Who may receive your information

We share personal information only where reasonably necessary for the purposes explained in this policy, where you have agreed, or where the law permits or requires us to do so.

Recipients may include:

Payment providers: Stripe and any other payment provider actually offered at checkout, for payment processing, transaction verification, refunds and dispute handling.

Website hosting and form providers: Netlify or another hosting or form provider used to host the website, maintain security, transmit enquiries and process relevant technical information.

Product-management services: Sanity or another content-management service used to maintain product listings and website content. Customer information will only be processed by such a service if the actual configuration or integration sends that information to it.

Delivery partners: Royal Mail, or another courier selected for delivery, to the extent necessary to fulfil your order and provide delivery updates. For orders to the United States, the customs details needed for import (your name, address and what the parcel contains) are passed through Royal Mail to US Customs and Border Protection.

Buy-now-pay-later providers: Klarna, Clearpay or another provider if that payment method is offered and selected. The provider may process information under its own privacy notice and make its own eligibility or credit decisions where applicable.

Professional advisers and authorities: accountants, legal advisers, insurers, regulators, law-enforcement bodies or other recipients where necessary for professional advice, legal compliance, fraud prevention or the establishment or defence of legal claims.

We do not authorise service providers to use personal information for unrelated purposes simply because they receive it from us. Their obligations and permitted uses depend on the applicable contract, privacy arrangements and law.

International transfers

Some service providers may process personal information outside the United Kingdom.

Where personal information is transferred internationally, we will take the steps required by applicable UK data-protection law. Depending on the circumstances, this may include relying on an adequacy regulation, an applicable UK-approved transfer mechanism or another lawful exception.

Where required, we will assess the transfer and implement appropriate safeguards.

If you would like further information about relevant international transfers and safeguards, please contact us.

How long we retain information

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, taking account of legal obligations, accounting requirements, disputes and legitimate business needs.

Our intended retention approach is as follows:

Order and accounting records: we generally retain relevant transaction and accounting records for six years where appropriate to meet applicable tax, accounting and business record-keeping requirements. Some records may need to be kept for a different period depending on the applicable rules and circumstances.

Customer enquiries: we aim to retain routine enquiries and correspondence for up to 24 months after the matter has been resolved, unless a longer period is reasonably necessary for an ongoing issue, a legal claim or another lawful purpose.

Complaints and claim evidence: photographs, videos and other evidence supplied to support a claim will generally be retained until the claim is resolved and, where reasonably necessary, for up to 24 months afterwards. We may retain relevant information for longer where required to handle an ongoing dispute, chargeback, legal claim or legal obligation.

Technical and security logs: these are retained for the period reasonably necessary for security, troubleshooting and operational purposes, in accordance with the arrangements of the relevant hosting or technical provider.

Browser storage and cookies: information stored in your browser may remain there until it expires or you clear it, depending on the technology used. See our Cookie Policy for further information.

Where information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete it or anonymise it, subject to applicable law.

Cookies and similar technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to support essential website functions, remember relevant preferences, maintain security and, where enabled, provide additional functionality.

Non-essential cookies and similar technologies will be used only where the applicable consent requirements have been met.

Our separate Cookie Policy explains which technologies are used, their purposes, whether third parties are involved and how you can manage your preferences.

Marketing communications

We may provide information about our products, promotions or wholesale offering where we have a lawful basis to do so and have complied with applicable electronic-marketing rules.

Where consent is required, we will obtain it before sending marketing communications.

Your data-protection rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access: request a copy of your personal information.
  • Rectification: ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Erasure: request deletion of information where the legal conditions are met.
  • Restriction: request that we restrict certain processing.
  • Objection: object to processing carried out on the basis of legitimate interests, where the legal conditions apply.
  • Data portability: request certain information in a commonly used, machine-readable format where the right applies.
  • Withdraw consent: withdraw consent where we rely on consent as our lawful basis.

These rights are not absolute and may be subject to legal conditions or exceptions.

To exercise a right, contact info@satcgvault.co.uk and explain your request. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

We will respond within the period required by applicable law. Where a request is complex or other lawful conditions apply, we will explain any permitted extension.
