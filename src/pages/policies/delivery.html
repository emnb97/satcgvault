Dispatch

Orders placed before 15:00 on a working day are dispatched the same day. Orders after that go out the next working day. Pre-orders are dispatched from the release date shown on the product page.

UK delivery

Royal Mail Tracked 48{{SHIP_48}}, free on orders over {{SHIP_FREE}}. Aims to arrive within 2–3 working days.
Royal Mail Tracked 24{{SHIP_24}}. Aims to arrive the next working day.
{{GIFT_POLICY}}

Packaging

Sealed boxes ship in rigid outer cartons with void fill. Graded slabs ship in fitted foam inside a rigid box. Every parcel is photographed with the label attached before it leaves us.

If a parcel arrives damaged

Photograph it before opening if you can, keep all packaging, and contact us with your order number. An unboxing video makes this much faster to resolve — see the unboxing guidance.

United States

We deliver to the mainland United States with Royal Mail International Tracked. The price depends on the packed weight of your order and is shown at checkout.

Up to 1 kg{{US_1KG}}. Aims to arrive within {{US_DAYS}} business days.
Up to 2 kg{{US_2KG}}. Aims to arrive within {{US_DAYS}} business days.

Orders heavier than 2 kg packed can't be checked out online. Email info@satcgvault.co.uk for a quote. We can't deliver to Alaska, Hawaii, US territories or military (APO/FPO) addresses; if one is entered at checkout we'll contact you and refund the order in full. Free delivery over {{SHIP_FREE}} applies to UK orders only.

US orders are sent with Royal Mail's duties-paid service: we pay the US import duty through Royal Mail before the parcel leaves, so there is nothing further to pay on delivery.

Other countries

We don't deliver outside the UK and the mainland United States at the moment.
