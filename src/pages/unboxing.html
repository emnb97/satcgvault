Film your unboxing

Sealed trading-card product is high value and easy to dispute. A single continuous video turns a difficult claim into a quick one.

Filming is not compulsory, and choosing not to film does not affect your statutory rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 or the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013.

What the video should show

  1. The parcel before it is opened, turned so all sides and seams are visible.
  2. The shipping label, clearly readable.
  3. Any tape, seals or security marks.
  4. The parcel being opened, without cutting the recording.
  5. Every item removed and shown, including packing material.

Why one continuous take

A recording that stops and restarts is much harder for us and for a courier to rely on. One take, from sealed parcel to contents, is what we and the couriers will act on quickest.

When we may ask for it

  • Damage in transit
  • Signs of tampering or resealing
  • A product missing from the order
  • An item missing from inside a sealed product
  • An incorrect item received
  • Any other delivery-related dispute

Sending it to us

Do not email large video files. Contact us first with your order number and a short description, and we will send you an upload link. Keep the packaging until the claim is closed.
