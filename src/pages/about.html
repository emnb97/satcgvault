Fakes and re-sealed boxes are the plague of Japanese imports. Everything below exists for one reason: so you never have to take our word for it.

One hop, no middle hands

Allocations come from licensed Japanese suppliers straight to our Manchester unit. No broker, no third warehouse, no repacking in between. Fewer hands on a carton means fewer chances for anything to be swapped — the single biggest thing separating a genuine sealed display from a convincing fake.

Every carton opened, every seal read

Consignments are photographed as they arrive and outer seals are checked against the supplier manifest. Boxes are never opened. If a seal isn't right, the carton goes back to the supplier — that has happened, and the record of it is in the arrival ledger below.

Inspect it in your own hands

In selected areas you can check the factory seals before completing payment. You can also collect by appointment from Manchester M2 and look at the box on the counter. Not a favour for difficult customers — it exists because the alternative is asking you to trust a stranger with three figures.

Dates you can hold us to

Every pre-order carries the supplier's expected dispatch date, shown before you commit. Release dates in this trade move — when one does, we email you before a tracking page tells you, and you can cancel for a full refund at any point before dispatch.
